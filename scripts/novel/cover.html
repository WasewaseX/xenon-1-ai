THE NINTH VEIN a novel of Ashkarad
In the salt-choked city of Ashkarad, flesh is currency and every heartbeat is a debt. Darius Mehran, a surgeon with a borrowed heart and a ledger of sold years, is pulled into a war between the Church of the Silent Chorus and the nine veins of power buried in human flesh. To save the girl with carbon scales, he must open a door that was never meant for a person — the Ninth Vein, the throne at the root of the body politic.
