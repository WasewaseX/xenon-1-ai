# Summaries — Chapters 54–59 (Arc V "Sister", first half)

## Chapter 54 — Two Circles Under
Ilay hears the deep music doubled on the morning Dar, Suri, and Vell return through the seawall gate: the Sister has risen into the deep salt vaults beneath the Throne shaft and lies there keeping time one beat behind Dar's pulse, while an older, deeper voice that was always beneath everything turns over in its sleep. The city is armed and frightened — the Index patrols with new pulse-readers keyed to the stock children's hymn-training (Auditor Seft's machines file Elias under HYMN RESONANT. STOCK. SEE REGISTRY — PAGE NINE), Mother Gavel's crews hold the Choratorium ruins and count clinic foot-traffic, the Open Counts have chalked their ward offices shut and gone to ground, and the wain is eleven days past the reef-city with the flats still scored GO HOME, NINTH. Closing hook: Roya chalks Ilay's new map — two circles under the city, one of them waking.

## Chapter 55 — The Woman from the Mud
At the dry tide-gates a salt-burned, barefoot woman walks out of the fog with a rooted black petal spread at her collarbone; the gate-reader prints ERROR: RHYTHM UNRESOLVED. MACHINE FAULT. (two rhythms, the second following the first), and Dar claims her under sanctuary terms — Old Tally's petal-token warming in his pocket like a compass finding north as she approaches. The clinic takes her in under darkened lamps (darkness feeds the root, light taxes it); she writes her first line quickly — ISRA. I BRING HER HOME. — and the eleven stock children recognize her as "two bells from one cast"; Suri enters her in the clinic book with no debt column at all. Closing hook: at night, writing slowly and with effort, Isra produces her second line, for Dar alone: SHE IS AFRAID OF HER MOTHER.

## Chapter 56 — The Burned Garden
Under Vahid's nine-line conditions (knocking is not opening; Isra descends as the only "door the Sister will answer"), Dar, Isra, and Suri descend at night to the salt-glass door — Roya holds the shaft head — where Tally's petal-token against the glass draws the deep's recognition in matched rings of frost. Through the Mind Vein Dar walks his memory-corridors past the four swept rooms to the furnished door he never built, knocks, and is let in from the inside: the Sister's self is a burned garden under a sky of falling ash, looping the First Vessel's world in flames, and she begs her first words to anyone in a century — "Tell me the garden did not burn. Lie to me." Dar answers with the only honest count he has (the city above counts its dead by hand; he came down 711 steps to knock) while the contact destabilizes: her ash begins re-filing his own rooms into cinders, the Lily clenches small, and he cuts the walk. Closing hook: Isra's slate — SHE SHOWED YOU THE MOTHER — and in the burning's center a white-gloved figure tends rows that will not take root, over and over, forever (a ring of old burn-scars at the wrists; not named).

## Chapter 57 — Who Kept You
The re-filing keeps running in Dar even after he is brought up and taken back down (Vahid: a contact cut at a distance is a line left open), his copyist rhythm slipping a quarter-beat and the burning audible in the deep music; Ilay goes down the shaft uninvited, past DO NOT COUNT THE HEARTBEATS, and hums the lullaby's second verse — the verse that leaked to her from under the world's door, the verse from the three-heartbeat face that no living person should know. The Sister goes utterly still and asks through the stone: "You are stock. Who kept you?" — then fills the vault: "WHO KEPT YOU?" Ilay answers honestly — "The Church did" — and the music in the walls goes quiet with something like grief (the closing hook); Ilay's music has stabilized the contact, the ash stops falling in Dar, and his two rhythms re-lock. Isra's slate renders the Sister's first outside-heard word for what was done to her blood: KEPT — NO ONE HAS EVER SAID IT FROM THE OUTSIDE.

## Chapter 58 — A Century of Witness
In the clinic cellar, with Ilay as the second channel (what the stone says, Ilay says) and Isra's root paying for every chalk-stroke out of her own warmth, the Sister testifies under seal — Milla transcribing, Vell chalking, Suri's ledger opened to TESTIMONY — THE SISTER: the First Burning of the world before (planted by keepers who did not know what they were planting); the Gardener's daughter — herself — locked above the deep as a living lid, with the amendment that the flight into the salt preceded the locking ("no malice in the locking, only arithmetic"); the nine little sisters sown a century ago ("Go. Find your hearts. And bloom."), whom the Gardener called her little roots. RECLAIM's true meaning lands: the bloomed cities are not colonies but feed — BLOOMED—TRUE THRONE means ripe — pruned city by city and poured down the deep roads to keep the buried Root dreaming (Suri reads her granary chit, PER THE RECLAIM — SUSTAIN THE ROUTE, and the slate confirms: the route is the feeding). Closing hook: her count is exact — two million, six hundred and thirty-one thousand, four hundred and eight heartbeats of the Root since the Burning — and last week, for the first time, it skipped one.

## Chapter 59 — The Locking-Collar
The Choirmaster receives Milla's certified copy of the deposition and confirms it from the Church's own doctrine — the Ordinal of the Undersong and the architect's plates under the Choratorium: the foundations are a locking-collar (COLLAR — MAINTAIN TENSION — SING TO LOAD), the harvest-machine was lid-maintenance, forty years of choir organs one long lullaby fed down the shaft; the sedation stopped when the organs died at the Blacktide, and the skip followed the silence. The factions convene under one roof for the first time — Mother Gavel (service the debt: rebuild the chorus, keep organs), the Index under Seft (seal the shaft, flood the vaults with lamplight, register the stock children, and file "the anomalous slip"), the Open Counts under Clerk Elara (publish the whole account to every doorway), and the clinic (Dar's motion: shelter the Sister, hold the door in trust, send the wain nothing, learn her terms). Closing hook: the debate dies mid-vote — runners arrive from the vault: the salt-glass door stands wide open, and nothing has come through.
