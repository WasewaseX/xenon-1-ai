# Summaries — Chapters 66–71 (ARC VI: The Soul Door, middle movement)

## Chapter 66 — Paid in Full
The Gavel serves its claim on Suri under the resold Chorus debt it bought for pennies (asset seizure; Mother Gavel wants her counting hands), and Suri corrects her own bill upward to the true retirement figure: 10,608 coin. Dar pays it by selling the Index a faithful copy of Milla's printout — Roya's thermal tracing — priced at exactly the debt, costing the city its secret: the Index verifies the two-rhythm pattern and will re-key its readers around it. Milla, furious at the unasked sale, reveals she has always held the true original (the first pull, with the machine's fault-strike and the destruction-order number) sewn in her coat lining — reconciling ch29's "only print" claim as a clerk's deliberate under-telling — and keeps it as the city's future proof. Suri burns her own salvaged page in the clinic stove after writing PAID IN FULL across it in ink (her ch20 echo); the page screams in Chorus tissue-ink until Ilay hums it quiet; the column reads zero, and she keeps counting anyway.

## Chapter 67 — Common Property
At the rig over the salt-glass door (cold room, two chairs, one pulse-reader, NO SONGS chalked by Vahid), Dar and Isra learn the shared room's rule: every session takes a tithe, one memory from each climber into common property — paid over, not spent — irreversible, unbuyable, held by all parties in the graft forever. Dar chooses to pay the memory of his mother singing (a faceless kitchen — her face was spent at the Blacktide), so the lullaby remains his but is now only the Lily's song, never again his mother's; Isra pays her brother's name (the brother who "married the Bride" to clear the family debt and left his name at the temple door, lost with the drowned port's books). The room's common shelf now holds both, each climber carrying the other's loss; the pulse-reader prints TITHE: RECEIVED. Hook: at the room's edge the Sister begins, very softly, to hum the lullaby's harmony — the mother's under-line, not the child's thread — holding what was just paid.

## Chapter 68 — The Pick Comes Back Flowering
Mother Gavel offers Dar the pact: her crews hold the vault-mouth against the coming heralds if the clinic signs the deep over to her as a mine (salt-glass, the dead First Vessel's renderable flesh, water-debt forgiven forever); Dar refuses — "You don't mine a patient" — and she files the refusal. Four nights later a Gavel crew owed water under the ration goes down freelance; pickman Sedge strikes the salt-glass door's exposed flower-keyhole once to pry the cut flower free, and the pick comes back flowering — grey frost-petals down the haft, all pointing down at the deep. Sedge cannot stop counting backward from one hundred and nine, and the count only moves at thresholds (the deep has opened an account: it bills, it does not defend; the Cartographer's DO NOT COUNT THE HEARTBEATS warning re-read as an invoice). Hook: Gavel studies the flowering pick a long moment and orders all crews off the vault — "until the odds improve" — keeping the pick in her counting-room.

## Chapter 69 — Sold in Bulk
To fund the final push (vault watches, chorus-free distillation, lamp-oil, grain, water), Dar opens a deposit account at the League's counting-room by the tide-gates — a registry counter whose presses route to the mother house at Lowmark — and begins selling small true memories; Vell of Brack stands counter-guard ("BRACK SOLD HERE TOO"), and the clinic wall fills with deposit-slips priced and priced again as the fleet's approach drives rates up. Suri keeps his ledger of self (SOLD vs BEARING, the load-bearing walls: her stitching, Ilay's hand, Vahid's "control," the Blacktide refusal) and warns him he is three memories from hollow; Vahid lectures the doctrine (minds hollow from the connections out — "you are selling joinery"). One counterfoil returns pre-stamped RECLAIMED — bought at the source before the caravan left: the Gardener is buying on the open market in real time and funding him. Hook: the cleared slips are not random — every one is a memory of music.

## Chapter 70 — She Is Buying the Lullaby
Cross-referencing the cleared slips (Roya maps their intervals: one spine, bar after bar), Dar understands too late that the Gardener is assembling the complete lullaby from his deposits — the locking-collar's maintenance song, the chorus that would open the deep from above while he opens the door from below. He cuts the trade and closes the account (account-closing fee, last coin), but one slip has already cleared: the memory of Vey pouring tea — which carried Vey's voice saying the word "Gardener" ("She takes hers with the leaves left in... The Gardener drinks alone") and Vey humming the lullaby whole. Hook: that night the Lily cannot remember the lullaby's ending — her hum stalls at a swept-room silence before the last bars (the Sister's under-line crosses the gap) — and Dar realizes part of the song was never his to lose: he held the ending in trust (Vey's careless mouth, the fused pool) and sold it; the only complete copy left is riding the Reclaim road in the Gardener's wain.

## Chapter 71 — Every Crack Sings
The faction war climaxes in one night at the vault-mouth: the Index serves a conscription writ for the sibling-stock minors "as sonar," the clinic refuses, and the children go down — the only uncounted direction — to the antechamber above the salt-glass door, with Roya mapping the battle blind from a listening-post niche by calling positions off Ilay's live hearing like bearings off a star. Gavel's crews come down the freight ways (an abandoned mine is a mine the Index owns) and the three forces collide; in the reliquary corridor the Choirmaster holds the gallery with thirty freed, once-harvested voices singing the corridor's re-tuned wrongness against the Index's hymn-bait, and his fire-axe. Hook: in the melee a Gavel pickman's pick kisses the salt-glass door beside the flower-keyhole — a hairline crack — and every crack sings the lullaby in a different dead language (fourteen-plus tongues, all beginning with the same four notes); all three armies stand still and listen as the lid begins to come apart into music.
