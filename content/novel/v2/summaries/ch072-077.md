# Summaries — Chapters 72–77 (ARC VI climax "The Soul Door" into ARC VII "Throne of Salt")

## Chapter 72 — Inventory of the Dead
Same night as the battle, rolling into dawn: triage at the vault-mouth is done by inventory (courier bruised, miner's hand, two Index enforcers, the pickman who kissed the door left with a dead language that "knew his name," thirty freed donors, fourteen stock children accounted for). The crack in the salt-glass door exhales a billow of the deep's absolute cold through the antechamber, and Mrs. Gable dies shielding the stock children — coat spread over the smallest three, storm-lantern kept under her body — asking Dar to count them out ("Fourteen. All present.") and filing herself; Suri rules her into the clinic book the Ashkarad way (GABLE — DIED SHIELDING; a new column OWED BY THE CLINIC: FOURTEEN CHILDHOODS), Milla files the death notice with the stamp-box refused and the cause written THE COLD — THE DEEP'S. Mother Gavel surveys the cracked door's leaking cold, lets the frost write on her hand, recalculates ("A mine is a hole with an exit. This is a hole with an entrance."), and withdraws to the quarry without apology — order through debt, postponed. Hook: alone at the crack, in defiance of DO NOT COUNT THE HEARTBEATS, Dar counts the deep heartbeats and gets three — one heart, three chambers, each beat returning in threes, and the deep counts back in threes.

## Chapter 73 — For My Grandmother
Ilay (lead POV) transcribes the garden's true song — every verse, the mother's under-line included, stitched from the deep's humming, the crack's dead languages, and the Sister's harmony — in her private notation (numbers, circles, lines), titling the finished score FOR MY GRANDMOTHER, a kinship no one told her and she worked out by ear from a stock child's graveside eulogy for Mrs. Gable. Dar refuses her a place on the climb (the door is shared between two; a third voice makes a chorus; "Bring nothing you cannot lose... You're the thing on that list"), and she re-files: the score goes to Isra (slate: THIS IS THE MUSIC. I WILL BRING IT. / CARRIED. NOT COUNTED.), with Ilay deliberately holding the last page out of the sewing. Vahid renders the five remaining chorus-free vials down into a single injectable silence — "a rest" from Shirin's own recordings — for Dar to carry down as insurance. Hook: on the last page, beneath the final bars, Ilay writes the score's only instruction: "the ending is a door. don't let them count it."

## Chapter 74 — The Gardener Names It (ARC VI CLIMAX)
The climb: Dar and Isra take the rig chairs over the cracked door and enter the shared room, where the far wall is the Soul door — a seam where two silences fit — and the Sister, who has spent a year learning their silences, opens it from the far side; the Eighth Vein opens not in either climber but between them, a graft, "a pulse with two addresses," and the old pulse-reader prints RHYTHM: HELD IN COMMON while the crossing frost makes a nine-point pattern, every point touching every other. Through the opened door the Gardener speaks at once through every bloomed thing in Ashkarad — frost, petals, the confiscated flowering pick, and the far Cow's song rolling up the dead route — the Lily straining back from her own words (through her, not from her). She names what sleeps beneath the salt: the Root, her garden's heart, buried alive at the First Burning, kept dreaming for a century; and above it her daughter, the First Vessel, locked as the living lid, whose death at the Blacktide left the lid held by songs alone — and she notes she has Dar's sold lullaby bars filed and "had rather not spend them." Hook: her three creditor's terms — plant the ninth, burn the garden, or rewrite the ledger ("no ledger in the history of keeping has ever closed without a life in it") — with the deadline: "You have until the tide forgets to come back." The whole city hears.

## Chapter 75 — Terms on Every Door
Three weeks after the naming, the harvest fleet camps on the dry seabed — not an army but an audit: clerk-tents in ruled rows, bone tally-frames racked like muskets, white-gloved auditors, ranks of the herald-chorus humming a collection-pitch, and the wain at the center like a reliquary on wheels; a saltstairs child's unauthorized bell, not the garrisons' jurisdictional argument, wakes the city to look. The out-riders post one page at the tide-gate and withdraw at filing pace, and among them stands the prologue's bone-masked captain, unaged — "the choir must be complete" — who then takes up a permanent station on the flats as "the seal." By dawn every door in Ashkarad (and the salt-glass door below, frost-texted) bears posted terms: the stock children entire and the ninth seed in its carrier as tribute, in exchange for a place at the pruning schedule's tail — LOWMARK. THE VIGIL. GULLET. SARN. WEEP. OSSUAR. HALLOWFEN. ASHKARAD — LAST — Brack absent, already PRUNED, PAID, and payment due "the next tide" that no longer exists on any books. Hook: the signature on every page is not THE GARDENER — it is "WARDEN OF THE RECLAIM, PER G." — bureaucracy all the way down.

## Chapter 76 — The Quarry Assembly
Mother Gavel (lead, first section) chairs the city's choice in her own pit two days after the terms go up, having reduced both documents to three products: the chorus (reopen the machine, live as ordered livestock — it has the market's advantage of existing), the bloom (plant the ninth, be a Throne — fed, free, and eaten last; "eaten last is still eaten"), and Dar's unnamed third way, the only item on the menu the Gardener has not priced — with Sedge counting backward (now at nineteen) as her live gauge of the deep. The debate kills the chorus (the Index's own clerks will not hold the stamp; Elara simply rereads the tribute line), the bloom dies against the arithmetic of ripe, and Dar fails again to articulate the third way, answering the Choirmaster's old question — what is the city for? — with "For keeping," which is true and will not close a sale. The vote stalls nine short ("Nine short. I don't round."), and in the resulting deadlock the deep heartbeat skips for the second time in a century, audible to every soul in the quarry, every candle flame bending seaward toward the fleet. Hook: in the silence after the skip, Elias begins to hum the true song's first bar — Ilay's transcription, unclaimed blood, no hymn waking — and no one stops him.

## Chapter 77 — The Consensual Draft
The vote failed and the city organized anyway: Dar unifies the factions on the only terms Ashkarad respects — work, price, and skill — striking three mandates (Gavel keeps the water-works and her crews' debts; Seft withdraws the conscription writ and points the Index's readers outward; the Open Counts keep the wall), and the third way is staffed, not sold. Suri takes the counted: a census of the willing on the clinic stoop, Milla registrar, Vell chalking the second copy, columns SING / FIGHT / HIDE, nobody drafted, Gavel crews signing behind Mother Gavel's own mark — two thousand one hundred and some counted by the third day. Vahid takes the makers: chorus-free medicine ramped to city scale ("bottling an absence"), vials by the hundred, and the ash-censer smoke built to the Vigil's recipe — a grey resonance-eating lid over the Saltstairs that Ilay renders as "it sounds like snow." Roya takes what cannot be mapped: the deep vaults chalked live through Ilay's hearing as an acoustic score, charting the leak, the crack's languages, the Sister's pedal-tone (one corridor marked DO NOT POST THE FLEET HERE), and STANDING ROOM — WHERE A CHORD CAN BE HELD. Hook: the census's first page bears Ilay's signature — a flower with nine petals, every petal connected to every other, the exact pattern the frost made in the rig: the chord, drawn by a child, above the heading ASHKARAD — COUNTED.
